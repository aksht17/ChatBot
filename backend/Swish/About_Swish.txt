About Swish

Swish was started in Bengaluru in 2024 because food delivery had a problem: the food wasn't good enough, and it took too long. We decided to fix both. We operate our own kitchens, manage our own sourcing, and handle our own deliveries. No third parties, no platform fees, no middlemen. That means we can focus on what actually matters: making food you want to order again.

“In your neighbourhood, at your door in ten minutes”


Neighbourhood Kitchens
Our kitchens are built right where you live, so food doesn’t have to travel much. 


Proprietary Technology
We built our own app, routing & kitchen systems from scratch. It means every order runs the same way, every time.


Owned Supply Chain
We choose the ingredients, cook the food, and send it out ourselves. 


Last-Mile Delivery
Our delivery partners pick up from our kitchens and bring it straight to you.
